Silver Award Winner

Urban Landscapes Collection

"The city speaks in a thousand voices, if we take the time to listen."
— Kobus van der Merwe

Capturing the essence of South African urban life requires more than technical skill—it demands empathy, patience, and an understanding of the stories embedded in every street corner. This collection represents three years of documenting the vibrant energy of Johannesburg's evolving cityscape.

Through careful composition and masterful use of natural light, the photographer reveals the beauty in everyday moments often overlooked by hurried passersby. Each image serves as a meditation on community, architecture, and the human experience in urban spaces.

The judges recognized this work for its authentic portrayal of contemporary South African life, noting the photographer's ability to find extraordinary beauty in ordinary scenes.

This body of work represents the future of culturally-informed design, demonstrating how respect for tradition and innovative thinking can coexist harmoniously.

Thabo Mokwena

Digital Artist & Illustrator

"Art should challenge perspectives and spark conversations about our shared humanity."

About the Artist

Thabo Mokwena is a visionary digital artist whose work transcends traditional boundaries between technology and human expression. With over a decade of experience in the creative industry, he has established himself as one of South Africa's most innovative illustrators.

His unique approach combines classical illustration techniques with cutting-edge digital tools, resulting in artwork that feels both timeless and thoroughly modern. Each piece tells a story, inviting viewers to explore layers of meaning and cultural significance.

Career Highlights

Featured in international exhibitions across three continents. Work published in leading design magazines including Creative Review and Communication Arts. Collaborated with major brands while maintaining artistic integrity and cultural authenticity.

Creative Philosophy

For Thabo, art is not merely decoration—it's a powerful tool for social commentary and cultural preservation. His work often addresses themes of identity, belonging, and the African diaspora experience, creating visual narratives that resonate across cultural boundaries.

Through his practice, he aims to challenge viewers' perspectives and spark meaningful conversations about our shared humanity. Every piece is carefully crafted to engage both intellectually and emotionally.

Looking Forward

Currently, Thabo is working on a new series exploring the intersection of traditional African art forms and digital innovation. This ambitious project promises to push the boundaries of what's possible in contemporary illustration while honoring cultural heritage.

Naledi Sithole

Graphic Designer

"Design is about solving problems while creating beauty that resonates with people's souls."

About the Artist

Naledi Sithole approaches graphic design with a rare combination of strategic thinking and artistic sensibility. Her work demonstrates how thoughtful design can solve complex problems while creating emotional connections with audiences.

Based in Johannesburg, Naledi has built a reputation for creating brand identities that authentically represent diverse South African businesses. Her designs honor cultural context while pushing creative boundaries.

Notable Projects

Brand identity for three major South African corporations. Award-winning packaging design featured in international competitions. Regular speaker at design conferences across Africa.

Design Approach

Naledi believes that great design must serve a purpose beyond aesthetics. Every project begins with deep research into the client's needs, audience, and cultural context. This foundation ensures that the final design not only looks beautiful but also effectively communicates the intended message.

Her process involves extensive collaboration with clients, viewing design as a partnership rather than a service. This approach has resulted in long-term relationships and consistently successful outcomes.

Future Vision

Looking ahead, Naledi is passionate about mentoring the next generation of African designers. She regularly conducts workshops and has plans to establish a design education initiative focused on underserved communities.

Kobus van der Merwe

Photographer

"Every photograph captures a moment that will never exist again. That's the magic."

About the Artist

Kobus van der Merwe's photographic journey began in the townships of Cape Town, where he learned to see beauty in unexpected places. His documentary-style approach captures the essence of South African life with honesty and empathy.

Over the past fifteen years, his work has been featured in prominent publications and galleries worldwide. Despite international recognition, Kobus remains committed to telling local stories and documenting the evolving South African landscape.

Recognition & Awards

Multiple international photography awards including World Press Photo finalist. Work exhibited at major galleries in London, New York, and Johannesburg. Published two critically acclaimed photo books.

Photographic Style

Kobus's signature style emphasizes natural light and authentic moments. He rarely stages photographs, preferring instead to observe and capture life as it unfolds. This approach requires patience and deep connection with his subjects.

His images tell stories without words, inviting viewers to find their own meanings and connections. The emotional depth in his work comes from genuine relationships built over time with the communities he photographs.

Current Projects

Currently working on a long-term documentary project exploring the changing face of South African cities. This ambitious work will culminate in a major exhibition and book scheduled for next year.

Amara Okafor

Motion Graphics Artist

"Animation brings stories to life in ways that static images simply cannot achieve."

About the Artist

Amara Okafor has revolutionized motion graphics in Africa, bringing a distinctly African aesthetic to the global animation scene. Her work seamlessly blends traditional storytelling with contemporary digital techniques.

With a background in both fine arts and computer science, Amara brings a unique perspective to her craft. This interdisciplinary foundation allows her to push technical boundaries while maintaining strong artistic vision.

Industry Impact

Created motion graphics for major international brands and local startups alike. Developed training programs for aspiring animators. Regular contributor to industry publications on the future of African animation.

Creative Process

Amara's animation process begins with extensive storyboarding and concept development. She believes that strong narrative foundation is essential for compelling motion graphics, regardless of the project's scope or purpose.

Her attention to timing, pacing, and visual rhythm sets her work apart. Every frame is considered, every transition purposeful. This meticulous approach results in animations that feel organic and engaging from start to finish.

Looking Ahead

Amara is currently developing an animated series celebrating African folklore, bringing ancient stories to new audiences through modern animation techniques. This passion project represents years of research and cultural consultation.

Sipho Mthembu

Web Designer

"Good design is invisible – it works so well that users don't even think about it."

About the Artist

Sipho Mthembu is at the forefront of web design innovation in South Africa. His user-centered approach prioritizes accessibility and usability without sacrificing aesthetic appeal. Every project reflects his belief that great design serves people first.

With expertise spanning UX design, front-end development, and visual design, Sipho brings a holistic understanding to every project. This comprehensive skill set enables him to create seamless digital experiences from concept to deployment.

Professional Achievements

Designed award-winning websites for leading South African organizations. Published thought leadership articles on inclusive web design. Conducts accessibility audits and consulting for major corporations.

Design Philosophy

Sipho advocates for design that prioritizes user needs above trends or personal preference. His rigorous testing process ensures that websites function beautifully across all devices and for users of all abilities.

He's passionate about web accessibility, viewing it not as a constraint but as a design challenge that leads to better solutions for everyone. His work demonstrates that inclusive design benefits all users, not just those with disabilities.

Community Involvement

Beyond client work, Sipho dedicates time to teaching web design fundamentals to students from disadvantaged backgrounds. He believes technology skills can create economic opportunities and is committed to expanding access to design education.

Keabetswe Motaung

Brand Identity Designer

"A brand is not what you say it is, it's what your audience believes it to be."

About the Artist

Keabetswe Motaung has built her career on understanding the psychology behind brand perception. Her strategic approach to identity design goes far beyond logos, creating comprehensive visual systems that tell coherent brand stories.

Her portfolio includes work with startups seeking to establish their voice and established companies undergoing rebranding. Each project benefits from her ability to distill complex business objectives into clear visual communication.

Industry Recognition

Brand identities featured in international design annuals. Keynote speaker at branding conferences. Contributed chapters to books on contemporary African design practices.

Branding Methodology

Keabetswe's process begins with intensive discovery sessions, exploring not just what clients do, but why they do it. This deep understanding of purpose and values informs every design decision, ensuring authentic brand expression.

She creates flexible brand systems that maintain consistency while allowing for creative expression across applications. Her comprehensive guidelines empower clients to maintain brand integrity long after project completion.

Future Direction

Currently developing a framework for culturally-conscious branding, helping African businesses honor their heritage while competing in global markets. This work addresses a critical need in the evolving landscape of African commerce.
